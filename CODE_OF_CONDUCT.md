# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be useful, welcoming, and technically rigorous.

Participants must be respectful, discuss ideas rather than personal attributes, give
actionable feedback, disclose conflicts of interest, and avoid harassment, threats,
doxxing, spam, or discriminatory language. Good-faith disagreement about benchmark
design is welcome when supported by reproducible evidence.

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through the contact method
on their GitHub profiles. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public
issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban users
whose conduct harms the community. This policy applies in issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews, and project-related events or communication.
